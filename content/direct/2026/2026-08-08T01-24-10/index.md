Finally saw [The Death of Robin Hood](https://www.themoviedb.org/movie/1284465-the-death-of-robin-hood). Went in expecting something closer to [Logan](https://www.themoviedb.org/movie/263115-logan) (which I loved) and came away with something much less. The ending in particular felt flat, unearned, and just _sad_ (and not in a good way). I love the principal of stories like this; ones that don't simply re-tell what we've already seen, but go into the "after story" and explore the "what happened next?"
